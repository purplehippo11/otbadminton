Get Involved

Ready to play? Here is everything you need to know to join the Oakland Tech Badminton Team.

Meeting Times and Location

  • We practice and hold conditioning in the Oakland Tech gym
  • Exact practice times vary depending on who is available to watch over the gym that day, and conditioning can potentially be canceled if the captains or co-captains are too busy
  • Games against other schools begin in March/April
  • The Discord server always has the most up-to-date schedule, conditioning plans, and team communication

Membership Requirements

  • No experience needed, all skill levels are welcome
  • Join the Discord server to stay in touch with the team
  • Try your best to attend practice every day
  • Badminton counts for P.E. credit
