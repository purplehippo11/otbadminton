Get Involved

Ready to play? Here is everything you need to know to join the Oakland Tech Badminton Team.

Fill out the interest form

Meeting Times and Location

  • Practice and conditioning take place in the Oakland Tech gym
  • Times vary. Check Discord for the latest practice and conditioning schedule
  • Games against other schools begin in March or April
What can change the schedule?

Practice times depend on who is available to supervise the gym. Conditioning may be canceled if the team leaders are unavailable.

Membership Requirements

  • No experience needed, all skill levels are welcome
  • Join the Discord server to stay in touch with the team
  • Try your best to attend practice every day
  • Badminton counts for P.E. credit

Contact Information

  • Discord (fastest way to reach us): Oakland Tech Badminton Discord server
  • Instagram: @othsbadminton
  • Email: otbadminton@gmail.com
