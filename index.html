Oakland Tech Badminton Team

Welcome to the Oakland Tech Badminton Team! Whether you have been playing for years or have never picked up a racket, there is a spot for you here. Badminton offers P.E. credit, a fun community, and a chance to compete against other Oakland schools once the season starts. Join our Discord server to stay up to date on everything happening with the team.

Why join our club?

  • Badminton gives you P.E. credit
  • All skill levels are welcome, no experience needed
  • Daily conditioning and practice keep you active
  • Games against other schools start in March/April
  • Meet new people and be part of a team
