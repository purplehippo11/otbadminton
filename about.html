About Our Club

Our Mission

Our mission is to give every Oakland Tech student a place to learn badminton, stay active, and compete together. You do not need any experience to join, you just need to show up with a good attitude and try your best. We practice hard, support each other, and have fun doing it.

Typical Activities

  • Conditioning to build speed and stamina, so try your best to attend every day
  • Badminton practice and scrimmages in the Oakland Tech gym
  • Check Discord for daily schedule updates
When do we play other schools?

Games begin in March or April. Badminton gets lower priority for gym space.
